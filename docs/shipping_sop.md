# Shipware shipping SOP

**Demonstration policy only.** This fictional procedure uses synthetic shipments.
It is not a real company policy or a carrier service agreement.

## SOP-01: Overdue shipments

A shipment is overdue when its expected delivery date is before the UTC assessment
date and its status is neither delivered nor cancelled. Check the recorded delivery
date and latest update. Prepare a carrier follow-up asking for the current status
and an updated delivery estimate. Do not assume a reason for the delay or promise
a new delivery date.

## SOP-02: Missing tracking numbers

An in_transit shipment with a blank tracking number needs attention. Check the
dispatch record for a tracking number. If it cannot be confirmed, ask the dispatch
team or carrier to confirm it. Never create a tracking number or substitute the
shipment ID as a tracking number. Pending shipments may have no tracking number.

## SOP-03: Stale carrier updates

An in_transit shipment has a stale update when its last recorded update is strictly
more than 24 hours before the UTC assessment time. Exactly 24 hours is not stale.
Check the latest recorded update and prepare a carrier follow-up requesting a
current status. A stale update alone does not establish that a shipment is lost.

## SOP-04: Supervisor escalation

If the available facts or this demonstration policy do not provide enough guidance,
ask a supervisor to review the case. Escalate conflicting shipment information or a
tracking number that cannot be confirmed through the checks in SOP-02. Include the
known facts and the unresolved question; do not invent deadlines or penalties.

## SOP-05: Human review and scope

Delivered and cancelled shipments are excluded from exception checks. A shipment
may have multiple exceptions. Drafts must be checked by a person before use.
Shipware never sends messages or changes external shipping systems. This SOP does
not define refunds, compensation, or guaranteed carrier response times.
